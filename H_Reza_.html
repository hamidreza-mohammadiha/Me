    03 / Connections

    Interesting questions live between disciplines.

    I × II

    Strategic decisions under uncertainty

    How do equilibrium outcomes change when agents face stochastic environments, limited information, and sequential choices?

    II × III

    Policies tested in dynamic worlds

    How robust are optimized policies when simulated agents learn, adapt, and behave differently from model assumptions?

    III × I

    Mechanisms tested with participants

    Can interactive experiments expose differences between predicted incentives and observed strategic behavior?

    A unifying question
    How can we design decision mechanisms that remain effective when participants learn, compete, and adapt?
