01 / Research agenda

What I want to understand.

Research interests and possible questions, not claims of completed findings.

How can we design decision mechanisms that remain effective when participants learn, compete, and adapt?

I / STRATEGY

Game theory & mechanism design

Incentives, equilibrium behavior, strategic pricing, and coordination between self-interested agents.

II / UNCERTAINTY

Stochastic optimization

Dynamic programming, queueing, revenue management, and sequential decisions under uncertainty.

III / EXPERIMENT

Simulation & serious games

Interactive environments that reveal how theoretical mechanisms behave with humans and adaptive agents.
