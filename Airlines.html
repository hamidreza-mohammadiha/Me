Customer Behavior

Each customer arrives randomly and observes the current dynamic price, fixed static price, available seats, seat quality, and their own willingness to pay, urgency, and passenger type. The customer computes the utility of buying from the Dynamic Airline, buying from the Static Airline, waiting, or leaving the market, then selects the action with the highest utility. Customers who wait enter a spillover queue and periodically re-evaluate their decision as prices and seat availability change.



Dynamic Airline Behavior

The Dynamic Airline continuously observes elapsed booking time, seats sold, remaining capacity, expected sales, and booking pace. It updates its ticket price according to Dynamic Price = Static Price × Booking Pace Ratio × Pricing Coefficient, while enforcing predefined minimum and maximum price limits. The updated price is immediately offered to all arriving and waiting customers.



Static Airline Behavior

The Static Airline follows a fixed-pricing strategy. It maintains the same ticket price throughout the booking horizon, accepts reservations whenever seats remain available, and never adjusts its price regardless of demand or booking pace.



Customer Decision Set

Actions: Buy Dynamic Ticket • Buy Static Ticket • Wait • Leave Market



Dynamic Airline Decision Set

Actions: Increase Price • Decrease Price • Maintain Price



Static Airline Decision Set

Actions: Maintain Fixed Price



Customer Information Set

Customers know the current dynamic price, static price, available seats, seat quality, and their own willingness to pay, urgency, and passenger type. They do not know future arrivals, future prices, or other customers' preferences.



Dynamic Airline Information Set

The Dynamic Airline observes booking time, actual bookings, remaining seats, expected booking pace, demand arrival rate, and the pricing coefficient to determine its next ticket price.



Static Airline Information Set

The Static Airline observes only its remaining seat inventory and incoming demand, while maintaining a constant ticket price throughout the simulation.
