Whitepaper Architecture Specification

This application implements the simulation and optimization architecture described by HamidReza and Behrad (2026).

Poisson Passenger Arrivals Arriving customers follow inter-arrival distribution $\Delta t \sim \text{Exp}(\lambda)$.
Dynamic Booking Velocity Dynamic price scales via ratio $R(t) = B(t)/\max(1, E(t))$ with elasticity factor $\alpha$.
Heterogeneous Segmentation Leisure travelers (low valuation, high price sensitivity) vs. Business travelers (high valuation, low sensitivity).
Rational Spillover Queue Strategic passengers postpone purchase when expected waiting utility $U_W$ exceeds immediate purchase utilities.
