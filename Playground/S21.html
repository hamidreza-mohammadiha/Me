Whitepaper Analytical Framework

This simulator faithfully operationalizes the analytical models introduced by HamidReza & Behrad (2026)[cite: 1].

1. Stochastic Passenger Arrivals

Arrivals follow a homogeneous Poisson process $N(t) \sim \text{Poisson}(\lambda t)$ with inter-arrival distribution[cite: 1]:

$$f(\Delta t) = \lambda e^{-\lambda \Delta t}$$

2. Adaptive Dynamic Pricing Engine

Let $B(t)$ represent cumulative bookings and $E(t) = C \cdot \frac{t}{T}$ be expected linear booking trajectory[cite: 1]. Dynamic price $P_d(t)$ scales dynamically with booking pace $R(t)$[cite: 1]:

$$R(t) = \frac{B(t)}{\max\{1, E(t)\}}, \quad P_d(t) = \text{Clamp}\left(P_s \cdot [R(t)]^\alpha, P_{\min}, P_{\max}\right)$$

3. Utility-Based Strategic Waiting Mechanism

Each arriving passenger $i$ evaluates action utilities $U_D, U_S, U_L, U_W$[cite: 1]. The utility of rational postponement (waiting) $U_W$ is modeled as[cite: 1]:

$$U_W = \gamma \cdot S_r \cdot T_r \cdot \Delta P \cdot \left(\frac{V_i}{300}\right) - C_u - C_t$$

Where $S_r = \frac{C - B(t)}{C}$ is remaining seat ratio, $T_r = \frac{T - t}{T}$ is remaining horizon ratio, and $\Delta P = \max(0, P_d - P_s)$ is current dynamic price premium[cite: 1].
