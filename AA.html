Whitepaper Architecture Specification

This simulator implements the revenue management and competitive pricing framework established by HamidReza and Behrad (2026)[cite: 1].

Poisson Arrival Engine Stochastic arrivals generated via Poisson Process $\Delta t \sim \text{Exp}(\lambda)$[cite: 1].
Dynamic Booking Velocity Dynamic fare adapts based on pace ratio $R(t) = B(t) / \max(1, E(t))$[cite: 1].
Heterogeneous Segments Leisure travelers (low valuation, price sensitive) vs Business travelers (high valuation)[cite: 1].
Rational Spillover Queue Strategic waiting enables passengers to re-evaluate prices over time[cite: 1].
